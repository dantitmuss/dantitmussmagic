I · About

Hi, I'm Dan

I'm a magician based in New York City — cards, coins, the occasional borrowed object that doesn't come back quite the way you left it. I've also spent fifteen years on stages doing improv, sketch, and solo work: Edinburgh Fringe, UCB, The PIT, a short film that picked up Best Actor at a festival I still can't quite believe let me in, a monthly variety show in LA I somehow ended up hosting.

I'm also a member of the International Brotherhood of Magicians, which sounds a lot more official than it feels.

II · What I Do

Two ways to hire the impossible.

I.

Walkaround

Cards, coins, wallets, whatever's on the table — moving room to room, conversation to conversation. Built for cocktail hours, receptions, and anywhere people are already talking to each other.

II.

The 30-Minute Show

A tighter, seated set — heavier on mind-reading, lighter on the rest. Works well as an add-on to walkaround, or booked on its own if you want a proper moment where the room goes quiet.

Curtain Call

How to hire me for a few minutes of “wait, what.”
